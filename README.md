# Rocket Bot Royale: Owner Edition 👑

An action-packed 2D physics tank battle royale game inspired by *Rocket Bot Royale*, featuring fully destructible floating sky islands, rocket-jumping physics recoil, rising lava sea levels, smart AI bots, and a **complete Owner/Admin God-Mode Command Center**.

---

## Features

### 👑 Owner & Admin God-Mode Console
Press `O` or click the **Owner Panel** button in the top-right corner to open the admin command center with instant god-powers:
* **Invincibility (God Mode)**: Take zero damage from enemy shots or rising lava.
* **Infinite Ammo & 0s Cooldown**: Rapid-fire weapons instantly.
* **Moon Gravity**: Toggle low floaty gravity for massive rocket jumps.
* **Right-Click Teleport**: Instantly teleport your tank anywhere on the map by right-clicking.
* **Recoil Multiplier**: Switch between `0x` (No Recoil), `1x`, `2.5x`, and `5x MEGA` launch power.
* **Weapon Arsenal Override**: Instantly equip and fire *Standard Rocket*, *Triple Spread*, *Mega Nuke*, *Orbital Laser*, *Homing Swarm*, or *Black Hole Bomb*.
* **World Commands**: Call down a meteor shower, spawn enemy bots or supply crates, restore island terrain, lower the rising lava sea, add `+10,000` coins, or smite all enemy bots instantly.

### Core Gameplay
* **Rocket-Jumping Physics**: Fire your weapon to generate powerful recoil kickback, launching your tank across floating islands.
* **Destructible Terrain**: Explosions carve real craters and craters out of the floating islands.
* **Rising Lava**: The molten lava sea steadily rises from the bottom of the map, forcing all tanks upward.

---

## Quick Start / Installation

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari).
3. Press `O` to open your Owner Panel and rule the battle royale!
